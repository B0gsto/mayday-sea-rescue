# 08 · References

Curated references in one place: Roblox games to study, games outside Roblox for feel and art, real-world rescue references, and tutorials and tool links.

Link rules: every link comes from the research files in [`research/`](research/) or is a well-known official site. Marks:

| Mark | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **[R]** | Link taken from our research files (seen 2026-10-17). |
| **[O]** | Well-known official site, not opened during research. Check the exact page before citing it elsewhere. |
| **(unverified)** | Fact or number not confirmed against a primary source. |

All CCU and visit numbers are tracker snapshots from 2026-10-17 and will be out of date quickly.
