# 15 · Retention and the new player

Last rewrite: 2026-10-25. This file owns why a player comes back and what the first session feels like. Numbers
are in `docs/03-progression.md`; the loop is `docs/02-core-loop-and-mechanics.md`; the canon is
`docs/00-design.md`. The old season, storm and challenge plans are retired.

## 1. Fair rules

Retention is built on **wanting to see the next thing**, not on losing something.

1. **No paid FOMO.** Every rank, island, building and call is earnable. Nothing time-limited is sold.
2. **No daily chores.** Calls are unlimited. The daily streak reward and the six quests are gentle reasons to return, not
   a checklist that punishes a missed day.
3. **One missed day is free.** A longer break drops the streak back three days, never to zero. Away more than
   three days: a welcome-back bonus, +500 Cash, once.
4. **No countdown next to Robux.** No offer timer, no "last chance", no pop-up on join.
5. **No energy, lives or daily call cap.** A player can rescue all evening.
6. **No season pass, no reset, no login calendar.** Progress is permanent. Service Stars are endless but optional.
7. **A short session still pays.** Five minutes gets the daily reward and a call; an hour gets a rank or a build.
8. **No dark patterns.** No fake scarcity, no social pressure to buy, no gambling with Cash.

## 2. The hooks

| Hook | What it is | Why it pulls | State |
|---|---|---|---|
| **H1. The next call** | A new call every few seconds, farther as reach grows. | The core verb is the reward; there is always one more. | Live |
| **H2. The island grows** | Dock Lv2–5, hangar, sub pen, lighthouse. | Visible ownership; the harbour is a scoreboard you can walk on. | Live |
| **H3. Quests** | Six givers, thirteen quests, one a day each; the lamp chain ends in a lighthouse. | A reason to walk the hub and sail the outer ring; a three-day story. | Live |
| **H4. Daily reward** | A seven-day Cash streak, forgiving, with a cosmetic on day 7; one Collect prompt on join (with any offline crew pay), no world chest. | A low-effort return that does not punish a missed day. | Live |
| **H5. Album and fog** | Stamps, bottles, views, critters, wrecks, pages; fog lifts as you walk. | Exploration that remembers; the map becomes yours. | Live |
| **H6. Friends and crew** | Bridges to six islands, four-player crew, +10% Cash per friend. | The harbour is social; a friend's island is next door. | Live |
| **H7. Flex** | Gold beacon, rescuer monument, trophy hall, liveries. | Status that is earned and visible, never sold as a stat. | Live |
| **H8. Service Stars** | One per 30,000 Rep past Admiral. | Endless small goals for the long tail. | Live |
| **H9. The big call** | The capsized ferry, public and server-wide. | A shared moment; everyone gets a share. | Live |

The hooks are layered by time: a call (seconds), the daily reward (a day), a quest (a day), a dock level (a session), a
rank (a week), a lighthouse (three days), Admiral (weeks). No layer requires the one above it.

## 3. The first fifteen minutes

| Time | Beat | Target |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Spawn at the hub visitor jetty; Bosun; pager beeps in three seconds. | 95% of new players answer the first call. |
| 0:00–1:30 | First swimmer in the cove; ring; "+1"; drop-off. | 90% complete the first pick-up. |
| 1:30 | First Cash and Rep; the counters move. | The reward reads before any menu. |
| 2:00–13:00 | Tutorial chain: dinghy, two survivors, swimmer, wildlife; first quest pin and dock Lv2 in view by 6:00; Lifeguard at 400 Rep. | 70% are still playing at minute 10; 60% build or buy something; rank-up inside the first session. |
| 13:00–20:00 | The RIB and dock Lv2 are affordable; calls leave the cove. | 50% buy the RIB or dock Lv2 in session one. |
| 20:00–30:00 | First quest completed; first Album stamp; the map opens. | End with more island than the player started with. |

- **No cold open and no tutorial menu.** The first call is the tutorial.
- The first two calls are in the player's own cove, calm water, short ride. The first timer is generous.
- The second tutorial call carries one survivor even though the dinghy can hold two: the player learns that a
  jet ski has one seat and that a return trip is allowed.
- The first rank, the first build and the first quest all land inside thirty minutes. That is the whole
  new-player promise.
- A player can ignore every NPC and still rank up. Quests are a side door, never a gate.

## 4. The daily return

A player who returns for five minutes gets:

1. the daily reward prompt on join (one Collect, no menu chain);
2. the nearest call, still offered;
3. one quest pin if they want it.

A player who returns for an hour gets a rank, a dock level or a quest chain step. A player who returns after a
week gets the welcome-back bonus and a harbour that has not reset.

- The daily reward is the only login reward (one prompt on join, shared with the offline crew pay). There is no calendar, no daily mission list and no "claim all" panel.
- The six givers each offer one quest a day, so the player chooses a part of the map, not a chore list.
- Nothing is lost while away: no call timer, dock or Album progress runs offline.
- Nothing decays. A player who stops for a month can return to the same rank, the same craft and the same island.

## 5. Social retention

- **Six islands, one harbour.** Bridges make a friend's island a walk, not a loading screen. Visitors can walk the
  dock, see the build and step aboard.
- **Crew.** Up to four players ride together; each extra member adds 10% Cash, capped at 30%. The crew panel shows
  who is aboard. A crew is a session, not a clan.
- **Friends.** +10% Cash per friend on the server, capped at +30%. No Rep bonus, no obligation.
- **The big call.** The ferry is the server's shared moment: four or more survivors, everyone credited, the
  deliverer gets the survivor share.
- **The monument and the beacon.** The week's top rescuer is named at the hub; the top-ranked Lv5 island carries a
  gold beacon. Both are earned, both are visible, neither is sold.
- **No PvP and no theft.** A call belongs to its player; a visitor cannot build, buy or take anything.

## 6. The long tail

- **Coxswain** opens the submarine and the top reach: the map gets deep and the wrecks open.
- **Rescue Pilot** opens the helicopter and the Lv5 island: the harbour gets a beacon.
- **Commodore and Admiral** are the long chase: liveries, the coat, the honour board.
- **Service Stars** continue after Admiral with no cap.
- **Album completion** fills every island page and the hub map.
- **The lighthouse** is the one quest-chain building; it is a landmark, not a stat.
- **The trophy hall** is the Lv5 display; the gold beacon is the server-wide flex.
- There is no seasonal reset and no battle pass. A player who leaves for a month loses nothing.

## 7. What we watch

| Metric | Target | Why |
|---|---:|---|
| First call answered | 95% | The game must start with a job. |
| First pick-up completed | 90% | The ring rule must read on a phone. |
| Still playing at minute 10 | 70% | The tutorial chain is not too slow. |
| First build or first rank in session one | 60% | The tycoon hook must land early. |
| RIB or dock Lv2 in session one | 50% | The first meaningful choice. |
| D1 | 50% | The daily reward, a quest and a rank bring them back. |
| D7 | 20% | The submarine and dock Lv3 are in view. |
| D30 | 10% | Commodore and the long tail. |

Also watch: time to first call, time to Lifeguard, the RIB-vs-dock choice, quest completion by giver, crew usage,
big-call participation, and the share of players who reach the lighthouse. A drop in any of these is a design bug,
not a reason to add a sale.

## 8. Build order

- **Live:** calls, pick-up, dock levels, hangar, sub pen, quests, daily streak reward, Album, ranks.
- **In progress:** crew hiring (WP-15), tablet build mode (WP-14d), the phone-free UI (WP-23).
- **Next retention wins:** finish those three before adding any new meta. A living station, a build that feels
  like Lego and a readable UI are worth more than a new event.
- **Not planned:** season pass, daily challenge board, energy, login calendar, paid Rep, limited-time islands,
  storm seasons, leaderboard ladder, pet sanctuary.

The game is a harbour, not a treadmill. The best retention is a player who wants to see their island at Lv5 and
one more call on the horizon.
