# Reference Games: Sea Rescue / Emergency Roblox Research

Research date: 2026-10-17. All numbers were read from the URL shown on the date shown, via WebFetch or WebSearch snippets. Anything that could not be checked directly is marked **(unverified)**. Tracker pages were fetched from a non-browser client, so some pages returned only "no running experiences" or 404; those gaps are noted rather than filled.

Source key:
- RS = rblxstat.com
- RW = rowatcher.com
- RB = roblox.com game page
- BX = bloxodes.com, RT = rotrends.com, PA = profitable.app

---

## Summary

- **Firefighting Sim** (RS, seen 2026-10-17): 19,225 live CCU, 34,798 record peak (Oct 11, 2026), 22.2M visits, 92% likes. Closest direct analogue to a rescue sim, and it is currently very large.
- **Blackhawk Rescue Mission 5** (RS, seen 2026-10-17): 28,198 live CCU, 636.6M visits, 90% likes. Helicopter rescue niche, proven at scale.
- **Emergency Emden** (RW, seen 2026-10-17): figures conflict between sidebar and body (see section 3).
- **Niche crowding:** The Roblox coast guard / lifeguard / sea rescue space is many small, mostly unverified games. The only ones with visible stats are in the low thousands of visits, or are not verified. Nothing found has a verified CCU above ~5K. The niche is not saturated by a dominant title, but it is populated by several roleplay and MilSim games.
- **Name conflicts:** "Coast Guard" and "Sea Rescue" are both already used by Roblox games. "Mayday" as a Roblox title was not found; only itch.io and other non-Roblox games use it. "Coast Guard Sim" and "Sea Rescue Sim" are not exact-title matches, but near-identical titles exist.
