# 07 · Store page, thumbnails and trailer

Last rewrite: 2026-10-25. This file owns the public face of the game: name, icon, thumbnails, trailer and
description. The canon is `docs/00-design.md`; the live feature list is `docs/01-game-canon.md`/`docs/02-core-loop-and-mechanics.md`; prices are
`docs/04-monetization.md`. **Only show what is live in the current build.**

## 1. What the page sells

One promise: **answer a mayday, bring them home, and grow the island you come back to.**

- The first screen must show the rescue verb: a boat, an orange ring, a survivor.
- The second idea is ownership: a Lego baseplate island with a dock that is clearly the player's.
- The third is the world: a forest hub, six islands, bridges, a helicopter and a submarine.
- The tone is warm, bright and toy-like. Not military, not grim, not photoreal.

A store asset is a promise the game must keep in the first two minutes. If a feature is in progress, it does not
appear in the icon, a thumbnail or the trailer.

## 3. The look

- **Stylised and bright.** Chunky shapes, strong silhouettes, saturated sea and sky, readable at phone size.
- **Lego-style for new things.** New buildings, props and the islands are brick-built: studs, bright plates,
  clean edges. Boats, characters and the hub keep their finished look.
- **The hub is a forest, not a town.** Trails, camps, bridges, a lookout hut. No high street, no cars.
- **The sea is the stage.** Orange rings, flares, white hulls, a green baseplate, a red life ring.
- **No photoreal claim.** Do not write “realistic”, “simulator-grade” or “cinematic”. The game is a toy coastguard
  world.
- **No first-person cockpit.** Every capture is third-person chase, helicopter orbit or submarine chase. The
  build tablet is in progress; do not show it yet.

### 5.1 Personalisation

- Active four: **island**, **pick-up**, **hub**, **Bosun**.
- A/B two: helicopter vs submarine; ferry vs six islands.
- Question to answer with the set: “Which one makes a phone player tap Play?” Not “which one is prettiest?”
- Refresh the bottom three when the dock, the quests or the submarine change. Keep the top four stable so the
  page has a face.

### 5.2 Capture rules

- Use the real game camera; no free-cam angles a player cannot see.
- Real lighting, real water, real UI. No added lens flare, no painted explosions, no composited text.
- One subject per thumbnail. If the eye has to choose between the boat and the dog, the shot is not ready.
- No more than three words of overlay, and only if the shot is unclear without it.
- Keep the orange ring, the white hull and the green plate as the recurring colour cues.

## 7. Title and description

### 7.1 Title

`Mayday: Sea Rescue`

Keep it. It says the genre and the tone in three words. Do not add “Storm”, “Island Tycoon” or “Port Haven”; those
are old or misleading. The subtitle in the icon can be omitted.

### 7.2 Description

Draft, under 1,000 characters, live features only:

```
Answer mayday calls and build your own rescue island.

Race out by jet ski, RIB, lifeboat, helicopter or submarine. Pull survivors aboard, bring them home, and spend
your pay on a bigger dock, a hangar, a sub pen and a lighthouse.

- Six brick-built islands to make your own, joined by bridges
- Calls get farther as you rank up from Recruit to Admiral
- Quests from six islanders, with a new one each day
- Bosun the rescue dog rides with you
- Crew up with friends and answer the big ferry call together
- Free to play: every rank, island and free boat is earnable

6 players per server.
```

Rules for the copy:

- “Brick-built”, not a toy brand name.
- “Free to play” means the game is free; every rank and island is earnable. Do not say “everything is free” while
  premium boats exist.
- No “another boat gets there first”: calls are not a race against other players.
- No storm, no first person, no town, no cars, no pet adoption, no 16-player claim.
- No prices, no sale, no countdown in the description. Prices live in the shop.
- The first two lines must work on a phone without opening “more”.

### 7.3 Genre and tags

- **Genre:** Adventure, Simulation.
- **Tags:** coastguard, rescue, boat, island, tycoon, dog, friends, helicopter, submarine.
- No “Town and City”, no “Racing”, no “Horror”, no “PvP”.

## 9. What we measure

| Metric | Use |
|---|---|
| **Icon CTR** | A/B the dog vs the island. Keep the winner. |
| **Thumbnail play rate** | Which first-session shot leads to a play. |
| **First-call completion** | The store promise must survive the first minute. |
| **D1** | The page brought the right player, not just a click. |

A high CTR with a low first-call completion means the page oversold. Fix the page before adding more art.
