# M_Veh_GaleQueen
dims_m: 6.2 x 21.0 x -
Class: small island passenger ferry, steel monohull, generic design (not a replica), C2 2026-10-18.
- Length overall 21.0 m: matches RescueService.buildFerry's greybox Hull (60 studs = 20 m) within 5% and keeps
  HullKnock's 5 compartments about 4.2 m apart. docs/12 2.1 row said 40 to 50 m ro-pax (tune); the C2 brief set
  20 to 30 m, a small island passenger ferry.
- Moulded beam 6.0 m, 6.2 m over the D-fenders (greybox 16 studs = 5.3 m). Typical small passenger ferries of
  18 to 25 m run 5.5 to 7.5 m beam (verify: e.g. Scilly / Western Isles launches and 20 to 25 m catamaran-free monohulls).
- Design draught 1.75 m above the keel bar (greybox keel bottom 6 studs = 2 m below the floater); freeboard 1.55 m midships.
- Height keel to masthead about 10.4 m; saloon 2.35 m clear, wheelhouse 2.15 m.
- Passengers 96 + 4 crew (signage); 4 x 12-person SOLAS liferafts in GRP canisters (48 places + rescue boat capacity, verify).
