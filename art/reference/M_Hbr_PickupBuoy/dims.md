# M_Hbr_PickupBuoy
Pick-up buoy (batch HP2b, docs/12 2.3).

dims_m: 0.63 x 0.59 x 2.34

- 0.60 m A-type (round, inflatable PVC) pick-up buoy with a moulded rope eye on top; braided pick-up line over the side to a light 10 mm-class chain; orange.
- Row range 0.6 to 1.0 m diameter (docs/12, verify); real pick-up buoys are often smaller (0.3 to 0.45 m A0/A1), kept at
  the row's 0.6 m so it reads from a boat at distance.
- Floats with about 40% of the ball below the waterline; ATT_WaterLine = origin (z 0), ATT_Mooring = chain end, ATT_Pickup =
  the ring / eye a crew member hooks.
Sources: Polyform A-series and hard mooring buoy sizes (general knowledge; verify against a maker's sheet).
