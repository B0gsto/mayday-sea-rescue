Reference: grey seal bull (Halichoerus grypus).
- Eastern Atlantic bulls 1.95 to 2.3 m long, 170 to 310 kg (Wikipedia current); Lundy site says adult bulls up to 3 m, over 270 kg.
- Western Atlantic bulls up to 2.7 m, up to 400 kg (Wikipedia).
- Row says about 2.5 m; use 2.3 to 2.5 m for a mid-size bull (verify).
- Head width about 0.3 m (verify).
