# M_Veg_Kelp_01 to _04
Reference: forest kelp (Laminaria) for Old Ned's gully and Haven Head beds.
- Stipe up to about 1.5 m (Wikipedia, L. digitata); whole plant 2 to 3 m (Wikipedia).
- Row says stipe 1 to 2 m (verify). L. hyperborea grows longer and stiffer (verify species choice).
- Blade width about 0.3 m (verify).
