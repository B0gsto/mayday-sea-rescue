# M_Sea_Liferaft
dims_m: 2.9 x - x -
Class: SOLAS throw-over canopied liferaft, 12 person (generic, not a replica), C2 2026-10-18.
- Inflated diameter over the buoyancy tubes about 2.9 m (12-person throw-overs measure about 2.6 to 3.2 m; verify on
  a maker sheet, e.g. Viking 12DK+ / Survitec RFD SOLAS); the boarding ramp adds about 0.8 m on one side (free axis).
- Two stacked buoyancy tubes about 0.45 m each; canopy apex about 1.55 m above the tube bottom; ballast pockets below.
- SOLAS / LSA code: orange canopy, retro-reflective tape, boarding ramp and ladder at the entrances, lifeline round
  the outside, canopy light (verify: IMO LSA Code chapter IV).
Sources: https://www.imo.org/en/OurWork/Safety/Pages/LSA.aspx , https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liferaft ; photos in SOURCES.md.
