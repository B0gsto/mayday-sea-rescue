# M_Hbr_NavMark (port can, starboard cone, cardinals, isolated danger, bell)
dims_m: n/a
Reference: IALA Region A buoyage. Port: red, can/pillar/spar. Starboard: green, cone/pillar/spar. Isolated danger: black with red bands, two black balls topmark, white group-flashing light (2).
- Pillar buoys about 3 to 5 m tall (row, verify).
- Can buoy diameter about 1.0 m, height about 1.5 m (verify).
- Cardinal topmarks: two black cones; colours yellow over black (verify exact pattern against IALA E-200).
Sources:
- https://ialacolreg.com/en/iala/iala-isolated-danger
- https://marinesafety.sa.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0013/1108012/7-Buoys,-marks,-beacons,-signals-and-signs.pdf
