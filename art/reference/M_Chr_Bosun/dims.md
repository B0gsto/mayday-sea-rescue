# M_Chr_Bosun (+ fur cards, harness) and RIG_Bosun
dims_m: n/a
Reference: Newfoundland dog, black coat, 66 to 71 cm at the shoulder (05 section 4.1, row).
- AKC breed standard average height: 28 in (71 cm) male, 26 in (66 cm) female.
- Wikipedia height range 56 to 76 cm.
- Mass: Bosun is an adult male: use 68 kg (Wikipedia males 65 to 80 kg; AKC males 59 to 68 kg).
- Body length about 1.4 m nose to tail (verify).
Sources:
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newfoundland_dog
- https://cdn.akc.org/Newfoundland-club-flyer.pdf
