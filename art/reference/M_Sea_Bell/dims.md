# M_Sea_Bell (S1a)
dims_m: 0.26 x 0.26 x 0.35
Class: cast bronze ship's bell for a small merchant schooner, mouth 0.24 to 0.30 m (9 to 12 in), height with the cast crown
loop about 0.3 m; name and year cast in raised capitals on the waist band ("MORWENNA 1902"); 150 years under water:
verdigris, sulphide-dark crevices, lettering rubbed to brown bronze.
Sources (reference only, not shipped):
- Ship's bell: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ship%27s_bell
- Recovered wreck bells (sizes, patina), UK Receiver of Wreck droit reports: https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/receiver-of-wreck (verify)
Earlier board stub gave 0.35 x 0.3 x 0.35 m (0.3 to 0.4 m from the docs/12 row, unverified); 0.26 m was chosen for a
small (about 100 to 150 ton) schooner, whose bell is smaller than a large ship's (verify against a recovered example).
