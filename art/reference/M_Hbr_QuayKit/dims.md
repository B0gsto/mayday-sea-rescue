# M_Hbr_QuayKit
dims_m: n/a (modular kit; each piece is its own asset M_Hbr_QuayKit_<Piece>, sizes below)
Reference: granite block quay walls of Cornish harbours (Mousehole, Newlyn, St Ives Smeaton's Pier, Porthleven, Mevagissey):
coursed dressed granite blocks with a battered face, granite edge copings with a rounded arris, flights of granite steps
built out against the wall face and running parallel to it (no handrail), pier roundheads, rock armour at breakwater feet.
- Quay edge coping about 0.6 m deep, about 0.3 m thick, 1.0 to 1.5 m long (verify).
- Step risers about 0.3 m (verify; old quay steps 0.25 to 0.33 m), treads about 0.3 m, flights about 1 m wide.
- Block courses 0.45 to 0.55 m, blocks 0.8 to 1.5 m long (verify).
- Kit: quay top at the greybox quay level (map Y 4 studs = 1.33 m above the game sea level), wall foot 4 m below.
- Shore zonation on walls: black tar lichen (Verrucaria maura) splash band, green algae and a wet stain from the waterline up
  about 1 m, barnacles at and just above the line, brown wrack below (general UK rocky-shore zonation, verify per site).
Sources: https://historicengland.org.uk/ (harbour listings, verify), https://www.mousehole.com/ (harbour history, verify),
Poly Haven granite_tile_02 scan (CC0) for the stone surface. Photo board not collected this batch (open issue).
