# M_Bld_Harbourmaster

Harbourmaster's office on the Port Haven quay: 10 x 8 m, two storeys of coursed granite (irregular blocks, staggered
joints, recessed mortar, per-block colour, salt / lichen at the base; own 6.0 x 7.7 m tile) and a grey Delabole slate
roof with lead ridge rolls, five lapped eaves courses, lead verges, a banded chimney and tapered terracotta pots.
Dressed granite surrounds, two-tier sills and corbels on every window, a keystoned door surround, segmented string
course, iron gutter brackets, hoppers, collars and shoes. White-painted sashes and panelled door. The storm queue
whiteboard sits inside a rebated frame with a full margin; the brass HARBOURMASTER plate is 1.02 x 0.36 m with
150 mm near-black caps.
