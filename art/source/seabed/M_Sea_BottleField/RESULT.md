# M_Sea_BottleField (S2)

A six-piece harbour-approach seabed clutter kit: a green Bordeaux bottle and an amber beer bottle lying half sunk,
a broken clear medicine bottle and a salt-glazed stoneware jar standing in the sand, a four-bottle tipped-out cluster,
and a drift patch with five more bottles, a rusty can and glass shards. Every piece has a drifted sand collar;
barnacle clusters and filament-weed tufts grow at the glass line. The glass has a 2048 _HP source (hand-blown
waviness and sea-frost pitting) so the NOR map is not flat.
