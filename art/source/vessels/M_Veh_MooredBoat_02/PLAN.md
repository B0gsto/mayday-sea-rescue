# M_Veh_MooredBoat_02 plan

10 m GRP Cornish crabber, same pipeline/spec as _01. Hull: flared bow, navy topsides (Blue band), white boot/sheer stripes, red antifoul, stern transom. Forward boxy wheelhouse with raked windows, wiper, roof mast/radar/VHF. Aft open deck: bulwarks, cap rail, parlour creels, fish boxes, ropes, dhan buoys, starboard pot hauler, tyres, weed. Painted "PH 117" and "MORWENNA ROSE" both bow quarters. BANDS identical to _01; 25k small_craft, _COL, ATT_Waterline/Moor_Bow/Stern/Boarding.
