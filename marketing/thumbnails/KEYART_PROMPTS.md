# Store key art: image-generation prompts

The in-game captures (01-06 here) are references, not final store art. Generate key art with these prompts and use
the captures for the jet ski, lighthouse, ferry and harbour shapes. Thumbnails 1920x1080 (16:9), icon 1024x1024.
Keep the main subject in the centre 60% (Roblox crops on phones), no text in the image (Roblox adds the title),
no Roblox logos, no real brands.

Shared style line (append to every prompt):
`Roblox game key art, bright saturated colours, stylised 3D render, blocky friendly Roblox-style characters with
expressive faces, cinematic lighting, dynamic action, sea spray, crisp silhouettes, premium mobile game marketing art`

## Icon (1024x1024)
A cheerful Roblox-style rescue hero in a red and navy lifeguard drysuit and helmet, riding a bright red rescue jet ski
straight at the camera through a big turquoise wave, one arm reaching out with an orange life ring, a big friendly
Newfoundland dog with a rescue harness beside them, golden sunrise behind, huge white spray. Tight close framing, the
hero's face and life ring large and readable at small size.

## Thumbnail 1: the rescue
A Roblox-style rescuer on a red rescue jet ski pulling a relieved swimmer out of a rolling turquoise wave with a life
ring, a coastal harbour town with a red and white lighthouse behind, sunny day, spray frozen mid-air, motion lines
in the water.

## Thumbnail 2: the hourly Great Storm
A huge dark storm wave towering over a small orange rescue lifeboat with its blue lights flashing, lightning across a
purple-grey sky, the lighthouse beam cutting through rain, a capsized ferry with bright liferafts in the background.
Dramatic but not scary for kids: the crew are brave and smiling.

## Thumbnail 3: rare calls and the Golden Gull
A glowing golden seagull with spread wings caught in a fishing net on a rock, a rescuer reaching to free it, a gold
beam of light from the sky, sparkles, a "legendary" feeling, a sticker-album page with animal stickers floating at
the edge.

## Thumbnail 4: pets and the sanctuary
A cozy harbour animal sanctuary: a cute seal pup, a puffin, an otter and a sea turtle with little life vests,
recovering in pens; one puffin riding on the back of a rescue boat with a kid-friendly Roblox character laughing,
warm golden-hour light.

## Thumbnail 5: Halloween Ghost Ship (event, Oct 24 - Nov 2)
A glowing green spectral ferry drifting through night fog, lanterns, a rescue jet ski with a lantern livery shining a
searchlight, friendly ghosts waving from the deck, a crescent moon, playful spooky (not scary) mood.

## Tips
- Generate 4 variants each and pick the cleanest silhouette; check every pick at 30% size.
- If faces come out realistic, add `Roblox blocky character, simple smiling face, no realistic humans`.
- Bright colour tip: avoid all-dark shots; storm art still needs a bright light source and orange/red accents.
